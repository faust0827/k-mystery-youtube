# Video 01: The Korean Prince Locked in a Rice Chest

## Title

## Description
In 1762, Crown Prince Sado of Joseon Korea was dragged into a small wooden rice chest by his own father, King Yeongjo. The lid was shut. The lock was turned. For eight days, the prince remained inside — no food, no water, no escape.

Was Crown Prince Sado truly insane... or was he the victim of a political conspiracy?

In this video:
- The turbulent childhood of Crown Prince Sado under King Yeongjo's harsh rule
- Signs of psychological distress and the debate over mental illness vs. political fabrication
- The role of Lady Hyegyeong's memoir and its political motivations
- The execution method that avoided spilling royal blood
- How Sado's son, King Jeongjo, spent his reign rehabilitating his father's memory

The Joseon Dynasty (1392-1897) was one of the longest-ruling dynasties in world history. The story of Prince Sado is one of its darkest chapters.
