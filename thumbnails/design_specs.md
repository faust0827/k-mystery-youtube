## Design Rules
- Resolution: 1280x720
- Text: 3-4 words max, large font, readable on mobile
- No subtitle captions
- Channel mark "K-MYSTERY" in bottom right corner
- High contrast: dark background + bright text
- Avoid clutter — one focal point + one text line
