Korea outlawed shamanism for five hundred years. It never worked.

The law said shamans could not live inside the capital. So who kept visiting them? The wives of the elite. In secret. At night.

Because they weren't going for superstition. They were going for a sick child. A difficult birth. A dead parent.

The state had laws. The shamans had an answer. You can outlaw a practice. You cannot outlaw grief.
