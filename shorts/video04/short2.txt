In 1684, a shaman was caught inside the royal palace.

She wasn't just performing a ritual. She was wearing the king's own dragon robe.

For a Confucian state that had outlawed shamanism for two centuries, this was the worst possible scandal. Her sentence: exile to an island.

But here's what the records show. Every generation passed a new law against the same practice. You don't need a new law for something that has stopped happening.
