Which brings us back to the thing the laws could never touch.

In the tradition, a mudang does not decide to become one. The spirits decide. The sign is sinbyeong. Unexplained illness, seizures, hallucinations, nightmares. A body that medicine cannot fix.

The cure is naerim, an initiation ritual in which the person accepts the spirit. Refuse, the tradition says, and the illness worsens. Some accounts say people died.

Modern psychiatry has another name for parts of this. It also has patients.

That tension, illness or calling, diagnosis or destiny, is why the story never resolves. The state could classify shamans as lowborn. It could tax them, exile them, and write them out of official ceremony.

It could not answer the question they were being asked.
