Five hundred years of laws. Five hundred years of the same ritual, performed just outside the gate.

The kings of Joseon were not fighting a superstition. They were fighting the fact that, in the dark, when someone is dying or gone, people want someone to speak to the other side.

The state had laws. The shamans had an answer.
