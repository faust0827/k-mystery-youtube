## Short 3 — "Why Korea Couldn't Ban Shamans" (52초)

**[0:00–0:03] HOOK**
Korea outlawed shamanism for five hundred years. It never worked.

**[0:03–0:15]**
The law said shamans could not live inside the capital.
So who kept visiting them?
The wives of the elite. In secret. At night.

**[0:15–0:30]**
Because they weren't going for superstition.
They were going for a sick child. A difficult birth. A dead parent.

**[0:30–0:42]**
The state had laws. The shamans had an answer.
You can outlaw a practice. You cannot outlaw grief.
