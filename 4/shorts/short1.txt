In 1477, outside the gates of the Korean capital, a group of shamans performed a ritual.

Someone reported it. The court asked who had ordered it.

The answer: the palace had. The king's own wet nurse and a royal eunuch had gone there to pray.

The king's response was a single line, recorded in the official records: "Do not do this again."

Here's the part that gets cut from history. Joseon banned shamanism for five hundred years. And the palace kept its own shamans the entire time.
